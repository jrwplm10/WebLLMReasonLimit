ABOUT
Advancements in Large Language Models (LLMS) such as ChatGPT, LLama, and Claude, have resulted in widespread adoption by everyday users for a variety of tasks, ranging from content generation to decision-making assistance. However, LLMs exhibit notable reasoning failures, which can be problematic, particularly when used for tasks requiring high-stakes logical reasoning, such as medical or legal advice. We propose an interactive web-based platform, along with a series of illustrative and dynamic examples, to demonstrate apparent reasoning limitations of LLMs to non-technical users. By increasing public awareness of the shortcomings present in these models, we would encourage more considerate use of LLMs.
